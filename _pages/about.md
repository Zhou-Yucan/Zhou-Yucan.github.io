# 👩‍🎓 Biography

I’m **Yucan Zhou**, an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Tianjin University. I joined Tianjin University in July 2025. Before that, I worked as an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, from June 2019 to June 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from Tianjin University in January 2019. From October 2017 to November 2018, I was a visiting scholar at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research interests lie in **hierarchical image classification, long-tailed recognition, federated learning, dataset distillation, and trustworthy machine learning**, with a particular focus on learning robust and reliable representations and decision mechanisms under imbalanced and heterogeneous data distributions.

I have led a **National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Young Scientists Fund** project on long-tailed distribution classification and have participated as a key member in several major research projects funded by national programs and industry. I have published over **40 research papers** in international journals and conferences, including **AAAI, ICCV, IJCAI, Pattern Recognition, IEEE TNNLS, and IEEE TCSVT**, with more than 20 papers published in **CCF-A conferences or SCI Q1 journals**. Representative works include studies on long-tailed classification, dataset distillation, federated learning, and semi-supervised learning. I have also filed or been granted more than **10 invention patents** and have served as a reviewer for leading international conferences and journals, including **CVPR, ICML, NeurIPS, IEEE TIP, et al.**


My research interests include:
- Long-Tailed Learning
- Multimodal Learning
- Federated Learning
- Dataset Distillation
- Trustworthy AI
